SMS EXCERPT – Environmental Compliance (SMS-ENV-05)
- OWS operation must comply with MARPOL Annex I.
- 15ppm alarm must be functional; any bypass/testing arrangement must be sealed and logged.
- ORB entries must be complete, timely, and signed by responsible officer.
- Any alarm or abnormal discharge must be reported to Master and recorded.

Training:
- Engine officers to complete annual OWS/MARPOL refresher.
