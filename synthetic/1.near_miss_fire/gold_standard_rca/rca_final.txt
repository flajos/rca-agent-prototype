ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS (RCA)
Engine Room Fire – Near Miss

Methodology:
Hybrid Fishbone + 5 Whys

1. Incident Summary
On 14 January 2025, a minor fire occurred on Auxiliary Engine No.2 due to a fuel oil leak
from a degraded flexible hose. The fire was promptly detected and extinguished.
No injuries occurred and damage was limited.

2. Timeline of Events
03:30 – AE No.2 started (Engine Log)
03:42 – Fire alarm activated (VDR, Engine Log)
03:43 – Engine stopped (VDR)
03:45 – Fire extinguished (Engine Log)
03:47 – Partial CO₂ release (VDR)

3. Evidence Reviewed
- Incident Report (Chief Engineer)
- Engine Logbook
- VDR Transcript
- Maintenance Records
- Crew Statement (2nd Engineer)
- SMS Procedure ER-FIRE-01

4. Causal Analysis
Immediate Cause:
- Fuel oil leak from degraded flexible hose ignited on hot surface

Contributing Factors:
- Hose exceeded manufacturer recommended service life
- Visual inspection insufficient to detect internal degradation
- No automated tracking of hose replacement intervals

5 Whys (Technical):
1. Why did fire occur? → Fuel leak ignited
2. Why was there a fuel leak? → Hose degraded
3. Why was hose degraded? → Exceeded service life
4. Why not replaced earlier? → No age-based replacement policy
5. Why no policy? → SMS lacked specific hose lifecycle controls

5. Root Causes
- Absence of age-based replacement criteria for critical fuel hoses
- Over-reliance on visual inspections for safety-critical components

6. Corrective Actions
- Replace all fuel oil flexible hoses exceeding 7 years of service
- Update SMS to include lifecycle management for hoses
- Implement maintenance tracking with age alerts

7. Preventive Actions
- Crew training on early signs of hose degradation
- Periodic audits of safety-critical consumables

8. Compliance & Review
Actions align with SOLAS Ch. II-2 fire prevention principles.
